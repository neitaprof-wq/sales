How much does it cost to ship a copier?

The cost to ship a copier depends on distance, the machine's weight and size, how it is packed (blanket wrap or pallet), and whether you need liftgate, inside delivery or stairs. A desktop printer costs far less to ship than a production copier. Call (866) 216-7742 for a firm price, and see our copier shipping cost guide for the details.

How much does it cost to move a copier to a new location?

A local move of a copy machine to a new office, or even within the same building, usually costs less than long-distance copier shipping, but stairs, elevators and inside placement all add time. Ask for a copier moving quote with the addresses and building details.

How do I ship a copier?

  1. Lock the scanner and carriage if the model has a transport lock.
  2. Remove toner, the waste toner box and paper.
  3. Tape doors and feeders closed and wrap the machine.
  4. Ship it upright on a liftgate truck, blanket-wrapped or on a pallet.

Our how to prepare a copier for shipping checklist covers every step.

How do you move a large or heavy copy machine?

Never carry a large copier by hand or tip it on its side. Use a copier moving dolly or cart on flat floors, a stair crawler or ramp for steps, and a liftgate to load it. Read how to move a copier for the full process.

Can a copy machine be laid down for transport?

No. Copiers should always ride upright. Laying a copier down can spill toner, shift the scanner and damage internal parts.

How much does a copy machine weigh?

Desktop printers and small MFPs are often under 100 lbs, office floor-standing copiers commonly weigh 150 to 400 lbs, and production machines can weigh much more. Check the spec sheet for your model and include the weight in your quote request.

What freight class is a copier?

LTL freight class for copiers is usually based on density, meaning weight compared to the size of the packed shipment. Send us the dimensions and weight and we will quote the correct class.

Do businesses switch copiers when moving offices?

Many do. An office move is a natural time to return a leased copier and bring in a new one, while others move their existing machine. Either way, we can handle the copier removal, lease return shipping and new copier delivery. Our office move copier checklist helps you plan.

How do I dispose of a copier machine?

Most leased copiers go back to the leasing company or dealer, so start there. For machines you own, ask your dealer about trade-in or recycling. We can pick up the copier and deliver it to the dealer, leasing company or recycler.
