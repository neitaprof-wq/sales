How Much Does It Cost to Ship a Copier? 2026 Price Guide

Copier shipping usually runs from about $150 to $1,500. Here is what puts your machine at the low or high end, and how to get an accurate price the first time you ask.

Updated November 1, 2026 · By the USA Copier Movers team

Most copier shipments cost between about $150 and $1,500. A local desktop copier going under 100 miles typically runs $150 to $350, a regional multifunction device going 100 to 500 miles runs $300 to $650, and a cross-country production printer runs $600 to $1,500 or more. The exact number comes down to five things: weight and size, distance, packaging, access at both buildings, and how fast you need it there.

Below is what each factor does to your price, typical ranges by copier size, the add-ons to plan for, and the details to have ready so your first quote is your final quote.

What Factors Affect Copier Shipping Cost?

Every copier shipping quote is built from the same five inputs:

  • Weight and dimensions. A 100-lb desktop unit and an 800-lb production printer need different equipment, packaging, and crew.
  • Distance. A cross-town move is priced on labor and time; a cross-country or international shipment is priced as freight.
  • Packaging. Blanket wrap costs less than palletizing, and palletizing costs less than a custom crate.
  • Access at pickup and delivery. A loading dock is the cheapest to work with. Liftgates, stairs, elevators, and inside placement add labor.
  • Service level and timing. Standard scheduling costs less than expedited or date-specific delivery.

Why Do Weight and Dimensions Change the Price So Much?

Freight is priced mainly on weight and the space a shipment takes up on the truck, not just miles. A larger copier usually needs a pallet, banding, and sometimes an extra person to load it safely, and each of those shows up in the price. Have the make and model, or a shipping weight and dimensions, ready when you ask for a quote. "It's a big copier" is not enough to price accurately.

How Much Do Liftgate and Inside Delivery Add?

Two identical copiers going the same distance can still be priced differently because of access:

  • Dock-to-dock shipments, where both buildings have a loading dock, are the least expensive to handle.
  • Liftgate service is needed when either end has no dock, which is common for small offices and home-based businesses.
  • Inside delivery places the machine in a specific room instead of at the door, which adds labor time.
  • Limited-access sites with stairs, narrow doors, small elevators, or tight city streets may need extra equipment or crew.

If you need the machine carried up stairs or set in a specific room, that is white glove copier delivery. Our white glove delivery guide explains when it is worth it.

Is Crating a Copier Worth the Extra Cost?

Not every copier needs a wooden crate. It is usually worth it for:

  • Lease returns, where the leasing company inspects the machine on arrival and may bill you for shipping damage
  • International shipments, with longer transit and more handling points
  • High-value production printers with exposed panels, finishers, or glass
  • Long-haul freight where the machine may change trucks along the way

For a short local move of a sturdy desktop unit, heavy blanket wrap and pallet securing is usually enough and keeps the cost down. We will tell you which level of protection your shipment needs instead of selling you crating you do not need.

How Much Does It Cost to Ship a Copier Across the Country?

Cross-country copier shipping is usually palletized or crated freight. A desktop unit typically lands around $600 to $900 and a mid-size MFP around $800 to $1,200, with production printers at $1,200 to $1,500 or more. Liftgate and inside delivery at the destination are priced on top if the receiving office has no dock. Exact ZIP codes matter: a lane between two major metro areas can price differently than a rural delivery the same distance away.

How Do Fuel Surcharges Affect Copier Freight?

Fuel surcharges are part of nearly every freight rate and move with diesel prices. They hit long-distance shipments harder than local moves because more of the cost is driving. See how diesel prices are shaping shipping costs in 2026 and how gas prices affect copier moving costs. Booking ahead and allowing a flexible pickup window are the easiest ways to keep fuel from driving up your quote.

Standard vs. Expedited: What Does Faster Delivery Cost?

Standard freight scheduling, where the shipment moves within a normal multi-day window, is the most cost-effective choice. If you are up against a hard date, such as a lease-return cutoff, an office move-in, or a sale closing, expedited or date-specific service is available at a premium because it needs dedicated routing. Recurring lease returns are a good place to save: our quarterly copier lease return guide shows how a standing schedule avoids rush pricing.

Copier Moving Cost vs. Copier Shipping Cost: What Is the Difference?

A local copier move, such as across town or to another floor, is priced mostly on crew time, stairs, and equipment. Copier shipping between cities or states is priced as freight on weight, distance, and packaging. Many office relocations involve both. Copier Shipping Cost FAQ

How much does it cost to ship a copier?

Most copier shipments fall between about $150 and $1,500. A local desktop copier under 100 miles typically runs $150 to $350, a regional multifunction device $300 to $650, and a cross-country production printer $600 to $1,500 or more. Weight, distance, packaging, access, and timing set the final price.

Is it cheaper to ship a copier on a pallet or with white glove service?

Palletized dock-to-dock freight is the least expensive option. White glove service costs more because it adds inside pickup and delivery, extra labor, and placement in the room you choose, but offices without a loading dock usually need it.

Does a liftgate add to the cost of copier shipping?

Yes. When either address has no loading dock, the truck needs a liftgate to lower the copier safely to the ground, and that is priced as an accessorial. Tell us about dock access at both ends so it is in your quote from the start.

Do fuel prices change copier shipping costs?

Yes. Fuel surcharges are built into freight pricing, so higher diesel prices raise long-distance quotes more than local moves. Booking early and allowing a flexible pickup window can help.

Does crating a copier cost extra?

Yes. A custom wooden crate costs more than blanket wrap and pallet securing, but it is usually worth it for international shipments, lease returns that will be inspected, and high-value production printers.
