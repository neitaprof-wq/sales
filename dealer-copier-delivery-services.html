Copier Delivery Services for Dealers and Leasing Companies

An outsourced delivery team for copier dealers and leasing companies, handling deliveries, swaps, removals and returns so your technicians can focus on installs.

Get a Free Quote Call (866) 216-7742

For a copier dealer, every sale ends with a delivery. For a leasing company, every lease ends with a return. Both are logistics problems that pull people away from what they are best at. USA Copier Movers works as a delivery partner for dealers and leasing companies, handling the physical movement of machines in and out of customer sites.

Services for Copier Dealers

Warehouse-to-customer delivery

We pick up new or refurbished machines from your warehouse and deliver them to the customer, with inside delivery and placement in the room you specify. Your technician arrives to a machine that is already in position.

Swap-outs

On a single visit, we bring in the new machine and take out the old one. The outgoing unit goes back to your warehouse or wherever you direct.

Removals and trade-ins

Pickups of trade-in, end-of-service or repossessed machines, returned to your facility.

Out-of-area deliveries

When you win a customer outside your normal service area, we handle the long-distance delivery so you do not have to send your own truck. See nationwide copier freight shipping.

Services for Leasing Companies

  • End-of-lease pickups from customer offices, delivered to your remarketing or return facility
  • Consolidated returns from multiple customer sites, delivered together
  • Inter-warehouse transfers between your facilities
  • Off-lease deliveries to buyers of refurbished equipment

What Dealers and Leasing Companies Need From a Delivery Partner

A crew that represents you well

Your delivery crew is often the first in-person contact a customer has after the sale. We show up in the agreed window, protect the building, place the machine carefully and leave the area clean.

Clear communication

You get confirmation when a job is scheduled and when it is completed, so your team can update the customer and close out the order.

Flexibility on volume

Some partners send a few deliveries a month; others send a steady weekly schedule or a large rollout. We plan crews and trucks around your volume.

Regional coverage plus national reach

From our base near Richmond, Virginia, we cover Richmond, Hampton Roads, Northern Virginia, Washington, DC and Baltimore directly, and arrange deliveries and pickups in every other state.

Storage and Staging

When a customer is not ready to receive a machine, or a rollout needs equipment staged before install day, units can be held at our Richmond-area facility. See copier storage for details.

What to Send With Each Job

A short work order keeps every delivery predictable: the make, model and serial number, pickup and delivery addresses, the customer contact, building access notes such as docks, elevators and stairs, any accessories traveling with the machine and the delivery window you promised the customer. If an old unit is coming out, include where it should go.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work directly with copier dealers?

Yes. Dealers use us for warehouse-to-customer deliveries, inside placement, swap-outs, removals and out-of-area deliveries.

Can you handle lease returns for leasing companies?

Yes. We pick up off-lease copiers from customer sites and deliver them to your return or remarketing facility, including consolidated returns from multiple sites.

Can you deliver a new copier and remove the old one on the same visit?

Yes. Swap-outs are a common dealer job. We bring in the new machine and take the old one back to your warehouse or another address.

Do you install or service copiers?

No. We handle delivery, placement and removal. Network setup, installation and service are handled by your technicians.

What areas do you cover for dealer deliveries?

We cover Richmond, Hampton Roads, Northern Virginia, Washington, DC and Baltimore directly and arrange deliveries and pickups in every other state.
