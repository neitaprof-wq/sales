How to Move a Copier

Whether we move it or your team does, a copier should be prepared the same way:

  1. Lock the scanner and carriage: engage the transport lock if the model has one, so the scan head does not shift.
  2. Remove toner and paper trays: take out or secure toner and the waste toner box, and empty the paper trays.
  3. Pad and wrap: tape doors and feeders closed, then blanket-wrap and pad the machine.
  4. Use a stair crawler or ramp: never carry a copier down stairs by hand; use a stair crawler or a ramp.
  5. Load on a liftgate truck: a liftgate lowers the machine safely to the ground, and it rides upright and strapped.
