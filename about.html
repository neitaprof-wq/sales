About USA Copier Movers

An American-owned and operated copier shipping and moving company based in North Chesterfield, Virginia, just south of Richmond.

Updated November 18, 2026

USA Copier Movers moves and ships copiers, MFPs, printers and other office equipment for businesses, schools, dealers and leasing companies. We are based at 2208 Station Rd in North Chesterfield, Virginia, serve the I-95 corridor directly, and ship to and from all 50 states. Call (866) 216-7742 or request a free quote.

Who runs USA Copier Movers?

USA Copier Movers is American-owned and operated and is part of the Professional Moving Solutions network, which provides nationwide white glove and last mile delivery. Our crews move copiers every day, so the heaviest and most delicate machine in the office is handled by specialists.

What does USA Copier Movers do?

We pick up, protect, transport and deliver copiers and office equipment, including:

  • Copier lease return shipping to the address your leasing company or dealer provides
  • Office copier relocation during an office move, on its own or alongside your main mover
  • Nationwide copier freight shipping and copier transport
  • White glove copier delivery and MFP delivery with inside placement
  • Delivery services for copier dealers and leasing companies
  • Copier storage at our North Chesterfield, Virginia warehouse
  • Large format printer and plotter moving, printing press moving and forklift shipping

We ship every major brand, including Canon, HP, Konica Minolta, Kyocera, Lexmark, Ricoh, Sharp, Toshiba and Xerox.

What area does USA Copier Movers serve?

From our base near Richmond, we cover Richmond, Hampton Roads, Northern Virginia, Washington, DC and Baltimore directly, along with the rest of Virginia. Beyond that, we ship copiers to and from all 50 states and handle international shipments.
