MFP Copier Delivery and Inside Placement

Multifunction printer and copier delivery that goes past the curb and the loading dock, all the way to the room where the machine will be used.

Updated November 18, 2026

Get a Free Quote Call (866) 216-7742

USA Copier Movers delivers multifunction printers and copiers past the curb and loading dock into the room where they will be used. We plan the path through lobbies, elevators and hallways, can unpack the machine and remove packaging, and can take the old copier on the same visit. Network setup stays with your dealer or IT team.

A standard freight delivery ends at the tailgate or the loading dock. For a 300-pound multifunction printer, that is often where the real work begins. Someone still has to get the machine off the pallet, through the lobby, up the elevator, down the hallway and into a copy room that may have tight corners. USA Copier Movers provides MFP and copier delivery with inside placement, so the machine ends up exactly where it belongs.

What Inside Placement Includes

  • Unloading from our liftgate truck at the curb or dock
  • Unpacking from the pallet and shipping carton when requested, with packaging removed from the site
  • Moving through the building on dollies, including elevators, ramps and, where needed, stairs
  • Placement in the exact room and position you specify
  • Attaching accessories that ship separately, such as paper decks, cabinets and finishers, when they mount without technical setup

Network setup, driver installation, firmware updates and service calibration are handled by your dealer or IT team. Our job is to deliver the machine in good condition and in position so that step can start right away.

Who Uses MFP Delivery and Placement

Copier dealers

Dealers often send technicians to set up new machines. When those technicians also have to unload and carry equipment, they spend less time on installs. Handing the delivery and placement to us lets the technician arrive to a machine that is already in place. Dealers who need this regularly can use our dealer copier delivery services.

Businesses buying direct

Companies that purchase an MFP online or from an out-of-area supplier often find that the freight carrier only offers curbside or dock delivery. We can receive the machine and complete the inside delivery.

Schools, clinics and government offices

These sites frequently have delivery hours, security check-in or rules about where vehicles can stop. We schedule inside deliveries to fit those requirements.

Planning the Path

Before a delivery, tell us about the building: loading dock or street-level entry, elevator size, steps, door widths and the room’s location. If you have the machine’s dimensions, include them, especially for models with large finishers or high-capacity paper decks. That information determines whether the machine moves fully assembled or in sections, and which equipment the crew brings.

Removing the Old Machine at the Same Time

When a new MFP replaces an old one, we can take the outgoing machine on the same visit and deliver it to the dealer, leasing company or another return address. That keeps the copy room from holding two machines while you wait for a separate pickup. For relocating machines during a larger move, see office copier relocation.

Scheduling a delivery? Send the make and model, where it is shipping from, the delivery address, building access details and your preferred date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you set up the copier on the network?

No. We deliver and place the machine. Network setup, drivers and calibration are handled by your dealer or IT team.

Can you unpack the MFP and remove the packaging?

Yes. We can unpack the machine from its pallet and carton and take the packaging away when requested.

Can you take the old copier when you deliver the new one?

Yes. We can remove the outgoing machine on the same visit and deliver it to the dealer, leasing company or return address you provide.

Can you deliver an MFP up stairs?

Often, yes. Tell us how many steps there are and send photos so we can plan the right crew and equipment for the machine and the stairway.
