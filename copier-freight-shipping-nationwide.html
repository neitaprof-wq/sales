Shipping a copier across the country is a freight job with an equipment-moving job at each end. The machine has to come out of one building, travel hundreds or thousands of miles without damage, and go into another building that may have no loading dock. USA Copier Movers arranges nationwide copier freight shipping that covers all of it, from inside pickup to inside delivery.

Liftgate Service at Pickup and Delivery

Most offices, schools and small businesses do not have a loading dock. A liftgate is a powered platform on the back of the truck that lowers the copier to the ground and raises it to the truck bed, so the machine does not have to be lifted by hand. We book liftgate service at any address without a dock. If you are not sure whether a site has a dock that fits a freight truck, tell us and we will plan for a liftgate.

Inside Delivery and Placement

Standard freight ends at the curb or the dock door. With inside delivery, the copier is brought into the building and placed in the room you choose. That matters when no one on site can safely move a machine of that weight. Our MFP delivery and inside placement page explains what that service includes.

How Copiers Travel Long Distance

Palletized freight

For many long-distance shipments, the copier is strapped to a pallet, wrapped and moved on a freight network with other shipments. This is often the most economical way to cover long distances.

Dedicated or expedited truck

When timing is tight, the machine is high value or the route needs fewer touches, the copier can move on a truck dedicated to your shipment.

Crating

For production equipment or shipments with extra handling, a custom crate adds protection on all sides.

The right choice depends on distance, deadline and the machine. For more on what drives the cost, read how much it costs to ship a copier.

Preparing a Copier for Freight

  • Lock the scanner carriage if your model has a lock
  • Remove or secure toner, and remove staples and paper
  • Detach finishers, paper decks and other accessories so they can be packed separately
  • Photograph the machine on all sides and note the meter reading
  • Keep the machine upright; copiers should not be laid on their side

Our crew handles wrapping, palletizing and loading. Check with your dealer about any model-specific transport steps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you ship copiers to all 50 states?

Yes. We arrange copier freight shipping to and from every state.

What is liftgate delivery for a copier?

A liftgate is a powered platform on the back of the truck that lowers the copier to the ground. It is needed at any address without a loading dock.

Can the copier be delivered inside the building?

Yes. With inside delivery, the copier is brought into the building and placed in the room you choose instead of being left at the curb or dock.

Does a copier have to be on a pallet for freight shipping?

For shared long-distance freight, copiers are usually strapped to a pallet and wrapped. Dedicated truck moves may not require a pallet.

Can copiers be shipped lying on their side?

No. Copiers should stay upright during transport to avoid damage to internal components.
