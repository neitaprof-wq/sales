Moving a copier in Washington, DC is rarely just about the copier. Commercial buildings downtown and along the K Street corridor usually require advance dock reservations, freight elevator bookings and crew check-in at security. Many Northern Virginia office parks have similar rules. USA Copier Movers plans DC and NoVA copier moves around those requirements so the machine goes in or out on the first attempt.

Working With DC Building Requirements

When you book, share whatever building management has sent you. That often includes:

  • Loading dock and freight elevator reservation times
  • Certificate of insurance requirements and the exact wording the building needs
  • Floor protection rules for lobbies and corridors
  • After-hours or weekend move windows
  • Crew name lists for security check-in

With that information in hand, we schedule the crew to arrive inside the reserved window and bring the equipment the building expects. Street parking for a box truck in the District is limited, so dock access matters even more here than in most cities.

Office Moves Across the River

A lot of regional moves cross the Potomac: a firm leaving a downtown DC lease for space in Tysons, or a contractor consolidating Arlington and Reston offices. Copiers are usually among the last items to leave the old space and among the first that people want working in the new one. We prepare each machine, move it upright on a liftgate truck and set it in place so your dealer or IT team can bring it back online quickly. Our office copier relocation page covers how we coordinate with your main office mover.

Deliveries, Removals and Lease Returns

Dealers and leasing companies serving the DC market use us for inside deliveries to customer offices and for pickups when leases end. We can take returned machines to a local warehouse, our facility near Richmond, or a return address anywhere in the country.

We operate from North Chesterfield, Virginia, and run the I-95 corridor between Richmond, Fredericksburg, Northern Virginia, DC and Baltimore. That lets us combine regional moves, stage machines in storage and handle multi-site projects that span the corridor.
