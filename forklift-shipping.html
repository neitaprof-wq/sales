Forklift Shipping for Dealers and Businesses

Shipping a forklift or pallet jacks? We move them door-to-door on flatbed or liftgate and ramp trucks, anywhere in the U.S.

USA Copier Movers moves heavy business equipment, and that includes forklifts and pallet jacks. We work with equipment dealers delivering to customers and with businesses moving their own warehouse equipment between locations.

What We Ship

  • Forklifts for warehouses, shops and distribution sites
  • Electric and manual pallet jacks
  • Single units or several machines on one move

The Right Truck for the Job

  • Flatbed trucks: for larger forklifts that are loaded at a dock or with on-site equipment
  • Liftgate or ramp trucks: for pallet jacks and smaller units, and for sites where a flatbed is not practical

We match the truck to the machine and to both addresses, so tell us what loading equipment is available at pickup and delivery.

Door-to-Door, Nationwide

We pick up from your dealership, warehouse or customer site and deliver to the destination address, across town or across the country. Machines are secured and strapped for the trip.

How Shipping a Forklift Works

  1. Request a quote: send the pickup and delivery ZIP codes, the make and model, the weight and dimensions, photos, and the loading options at each site.
  2. Pickup: the machine is loaded onto the flatbed, liftgate or ramp truck.
  3. Secured transport: the unit is strapped down for the trip.
  4. Delivery: the forklift or pallet jack is unloaded at the destination.

Forklift Shipping FAQ

How much does forklift shipping cost?

It depends on distance, the forklift's weight and size, the truck type, and the loading options at each site. Call (866) 216-7742 or request a quote for a firm price.

Do you ship forklifts on a flatbed?

Yes. Larger forklifts usually ship on a flatbed. Pallet jacks and smaller units can ship on a liftgate or ramp truck.

Is forklift shipping door-to-door?

Yes. We pick up from your location and deliver to the destination address.

Do you ship forklifts nationwide?

Yes. We ship forklifts and pallet jacks anywhere in the U.S.

What information do you need to quote shipping a forklift?

The pickup and delivery ZIP codes, the make and model, the weight and dimensions, photos, and whether there is a loading dock or loading equipment at each site.

Can you ship pallet jacks with other equipment?

Yes. Tell us everything that is moving when you request a quote, and we will plan it as one shipment where possible.
