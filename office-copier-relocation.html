Office Copier Relocation During an Office Move

A dedicated copier crew for your office move, so the heaviest and most delicate machine on the floor is handled by people who move copiers every day.

USA Copier Movers provides a dedicated copier crew for office moves, working alongside your main mover or on its own. We coordinate dock times, elevator use and truck loading, move copiers before or after everything else, handle moves between floors, and place and level each machine so your IT team or dealer can reconnect it.

General office movers are good at desks, files and boxes. A production copier or a floor-standing multifunction printer is a different kind of load: it is heavy, top-heavy, full of moving parts and sensitive to tipping. Many office movers and building managers would rather leave copiers to a specialist. USA Copier Movers works alongside your main mover, or on its own, to relocate copiers as part of an office move.

Where Copier Relocation Fits in Your Move Timeline

Two to four weeks before

List every copier and MFP that is moving, with make, model, current location and destination room. Check your lease for an equipment-location clause; some leases require notice to the leasing company before a machine changes address. Let your dealer know so they can plan any service visit at the new site.

One week before

Confirm dock and elevator reservations at both buildings and share them with us. Decide whether copiers move on the same day as everything else or on a separate day. Moving them first means they are ready when staff arrive; moving them last keeps them in use until the end.

Move day

Our crew prepares each machine for transport, which typically includes locking the scanner where the model has one, securing toner, removing finishers and paper decks and wrapping the panels. Machines travel upright in a liftgate truck and are reassembled and placed in the rooms you marked on the floor plan.

After the move

Your IT team or dealer reconnects the copier to the network and runs any needed setup. We leave each machine level, in position and ready for that step.

Working With Your Main Office Mover

We coordinate directly with the moving company handling the rest of your office. That usually means sharing the dock schedule, agreeing on who uses the freight elevator when and making sure copiers are not buried behind furniture on the truck. If you prefer, copiers can travel on our truck entirely, separate from the rest of the move.

Moves Within the Same Building

Not every relocation leaves the building. Companies regularly shift departments between floors or consolidate into fewer suites. In-building copier moves still need elevator time, floor protection and a crew that knows how to roll a heavy machine across thresholds without damage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should copiers be moved before or after everything else?

Either works. Moving them first means they are in place when staff arrive. Moving them last keeps them available at the old office until the end. We schedule around your preference.

Do I need to tell my leasing company before moving a leased copier?

Many leases include an equipment-location clause that requires notice before a machine moves. Check your lease or ask the leasing company before move day.

Will the copier be ready to use after the move?

We place the copier in position and level it. Your IT team or dealer then reconnects it to the network and handles any setup or service.

Do you move copiers between floors in the same building?

Yes. In-building moves between floors or suites are a common part of our work.
