Hampton Roads is a spread-out market. A single company might have a location at Town Center in Virginia Beach, another near Ghent in Norfolk and a third across the water in Newport News. Moving copiers between those sites means planning around tunnels, bridges and traffic windows, not just loading a truck. USA Copier Movers handles copier moving across the region and connects it to Richmond and the rest of the country along I-64.

Hampton Roads Areas We Serve

  • Virginia Beach: Town Center, the Oceanfront, Lynnhaven, Kempsville and Princess Anne
  • Norfolk: downtown, Ghent, the Norfolk Airport area and the Military Highway corridor
  • Southside: Chesapeake (including Greenbrier), Portsmouth and Suffolk
  • The Peninsula: Newport News, Hampton, Yorktown and Williamsburg

Planning Around Tunnels and Bridges

Crossings like the Hampton Roads Bridge-Tunnel and the Monitor-Merrimac can back up quickly, and a copier move that crosses the water needs realistic timing. We build that into the schedule and set arrival windows that account for it. If your building has strict dock hours, let us know so the crew can arrive when the dock is actually open.

Typical Copier Jobs in Virginia Beach and Norfolk

Office relocations

When a business moves between cities in the region, the copier is often the heaviest single item. We prepare it, move it upright on a liftgate truck and set it in the new space so your IT team or dealer can reconnect it. See our page on office copier relocation for how we fit into a larger move.

Dealer deliveries

Copier dealers serving Hampton Roads use us to deliver new and refurbished machines to customer sites, with inside delivery to the room where the machine will live.

Removals and lease returns

When a lease ends or a machine is replaced, we pick it up and take it where the dealer or leasing company directs, whether that is a local warehouse or a return address in another state.

Government contractors and medical offices

The region has many contractor offices and medical practices with check-in procedures, escort requirements or limited delivery hours. Share those rules when you book and we plan the crew’s arrival around them.

Connected to Richmond and Beyond

Our base is in North Chesterfield, near Richmond, and I-64 links it directly to Hampton Roads. That makes moves between Richmond and the coast straightforward, and it means machines can be held at our Richmond-area facility when a new site is not ready.
