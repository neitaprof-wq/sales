When a copier lease ends, the machine usually has to go back to a specific warehouse or remarketing facility, sometimes hundreds of miles away. The leasing company expects it by a deadline, and the office simply wants it gone so a new unit can take its place. USA Copier Movers handles that return leg: we pick the copier up from inside your office and deliver it to the return address you are given.

How a Copier Lease Return Works

  1. Get the return details. Your leasing company or dealer issues a return authorization (often called an RA number) and the exact return address. Ask for any receiving hours or labeling rules at the same time.
  2. Send us the job. Share the copier make and model, your pickup address, floor and elevator details, the return address, the RA number and your deadline.
  3. Prepare the machine. Record the final meter reading, take photos of all sides and remove personal items such as staples, paper and anything left in the output tray. Your leasing paperwork may list other steps.
  4. Pickup. Our crew removes the copier from its spot, protects the panels, secures loose components and loads it upright on a liftgate truck.
  5. Delivery to the return site. The machine is delivered to the leasing company or dealer receiving dock with the RA number attached, and you receive confirmation of delivery.

Why the Deadline Matters

Many leases keep billing until the machine is received at the return address, not when it leaves your office. Late returns can trigger extra monthly payments or fees under the lease terms. Booking pickup one to two weeks before your deadline gives room for building scheduling, weather and long-distance transit. If you are already close to the deadline, call us and we will look at the fastest available option.

Single Returns and Bulk Returns

A small office returning one copier and a company returning machines from a dozen branches have very different needs. For single units we schedule a direct pickup. For multi-site returns, we can collect machines from each location, consolidate them and deliver them together, which keeps the receiving warehouse from handling a stream of separate trucks. Dealers and leasing companies that manage returns on a regular schedule can set that up as an ongoing program through our dealer copier delivery services.

Protecting the Machine on the Way Back

Leasing companies inspect returned equipment, and damage in transit can become a dispute. We keep the copier upright, wrap exposed surfaces, secure doors and trays and load it with straps rather than stacking freight on top. Your own photos taken before pickup, plus the delivery confirmation, give you a clear record of the machine’s condition at each step.
