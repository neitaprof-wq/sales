Common Questions

Copier Shipping & Lease Return FAQ

Answers to what customers ask us most, including quarterly copier lease returns.

How much does it cost to ship a copier?

Cost depends on distance, weight, crating needs, and whether you need liftgate or inside delivery. Call (866) 216-7742 or request a free quote and we'll give you a firm price before you book. Our copier shipping cost guide explains every pricing factor.

Do you handle quarterly copier lease returns?

Yes — we specialize in scheduled, recurring lease-return shipments for dealers and end users. Email copierleasereturn@highvalueproductshipping.com to set up a standing quarterly pickup.

Which copier brands can you ship?

All major brands — Canon, Xerox, Ricoh, Konica Minolta, Sharp, Kyocera, Toshiba, HP, and Lexmark — in any size, from desktop units to production printers.

How should I prepare my copier before pickup?

Wipe the internal hard drive, remove toner and staple cartridges, and engage the scanner lock if your model has one. We handle wrapping, palletizing, and loading. See our full copier prep checklist.

Do you provide packaging and crating?

Yes. Every machine is wrapped, padded, and secured before it leaves the building, and crated when the equipment or lease return terms require it.

Can you pick up or deliver inside the building?

Yes. White-glove liftgate service and inside pickup or placement are available for offices without a loading dock. Let us know about stairs, elevators, or tight doorways when you request a quote.

How long does it take to ship a copier?

It depends on distance and service level. We confirm your pickup window and an estimated delivery date with your quote, and expedited scheduling is available for deadline-driven moves.

Do you work with leasing companies and dealers?

Yes — we support copier dealers and leasing companies with ongoing, multi-unit shipping schedules, not just one-time moves.

Do you ship copiers internationally?

Yes — in addition to nationwide U.S. delivery, we handle international copier shipments, including export crating and freight-forwarder coordination. Read domestic vs. international copier shipping for details.
