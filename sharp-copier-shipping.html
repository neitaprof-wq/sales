Sharp Copier Shipping FAQ

How long does it take to ship a Sharp copier?

Most domestic shipments deliver in about 2–7 business days depending on distance; expedited scheduling is available for deadlines. International shipments take longer — see our export shipping guide.

Should I ship the copier on its stand or cabinet?

Floor-standing units usually ship on their base, secured to a pallet. Loose cabinets and stands are wrapped separately.

Do you provide crating?

Yes — custom crating for long-haul, international, or high-value units, and pallet wrapping for most domestic moves.
